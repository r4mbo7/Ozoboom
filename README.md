# Ozoboom ![](https://visitor-badge.laobi.icu/badge?page_id=r4mbo7.Ozoboom)

A co-op survival game set at a psytrance festival. One to four friends defend the main stage's sound system against ever stronger waves of bad vibes, each with their own class, from sunset to sunrise.

**Play the demo: [r4mbo7.github.io/Ozoboom](https://r4mbo7.github.io/Ozoboom/)**. Free, in the browser, no account, nothing to install.

## The demo

- **The Main stage**, on the lakeshore, at 145 BPM: a whole set, two drops and their bosses, under a light that turns from dusk to night, dawn and day.
- **Three classes**: the Luxiole sweeps the crowd from afar with glowing poi, the Nounours holds the line and draws the bad vibes in, the Hygie heals, repairs the stage and gets friends back on their feet.
- **Everything falls on the beat**: the core pulses on the kick, traps fire on the beat, waves arrive on the bar, the boss on the drop.
- **What it takes to last until morning**: traps made of festival gear, extra speakers to plug in, circus props, boss relics.
- **Alone or with 2 to 4 friends**, on one screen or online, with keyboard and mouse, a gamepad or a phone.

The full game, with more stages, will come out on Steam. The name comes from Ozora and Boom, two psytrance festivals.

## Playing together

- **On one screen**: "Play together" opens the lobby. Keyboard and mouse take a seat with Enter, each gamepad with A; everyone picks a name and a class, and the first player starts the set.
- **Online**: in the lobby, "Play online" then "Create a room" gives a six-character code and a link to copy. Friends open the link or type the code, enter their name and class, and the host starts the set. No account, browser to browser; everyone needs the same version of the game.

## Playing on a phone

With your finger, alone or online, preferably held sideways. A thumb on the arena brings up a joystick under it; aiming and shooting are automatic, at the closest bad vibe in range. Tap a trap's tile to drop it at your feet, or drag it onto the arena to drop it under your finger. Tap the skill's tile to use it, and the ❚❚ button in the top bar to pause.

To play without the browser bars, add the game to your home screen (Safari: Share then "Add to Home Screen"; Chrome: menu then "Install app") and launch it from its icon.
